## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Data Analyst** focused on transforming raw data into clear, actionable business insights.

I work across the full analytics lifecycle — from **data cleaning and modeling** to **KPI development, dashboard design, exploratory analysis, automation, and business recommendations**.

> **My approach:** Understand the business question → prepare the data → analyze the drivers → communicate the insight → support the decision.
